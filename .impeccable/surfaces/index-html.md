## Direction contract

THESIS: Refuses the generic "escritório de advocacia" navy-and-gold marketing page; this is a trustworthy, fast fintech-style checkout — the transaction itself is the design, not a sales pitch around it.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white paper ground, graphite-black ink, one confident indigo accent carrying every primary action, active step, and focus state. IBM Plex Sans throughout. Cards with soft shadow, generous radius, restrained palette (neutrals + one accent).

STORY: The advogado understands in one glance this is a real, safe transaction — not a lead-gen funnel. Each step asks for exactly one coherent thing; nothing to doubt or re-read.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Step label + 3-dot progress indicator top center; single card, ~480px wide, centered; step 1 fields (nome, CPF, OAB) stacked with generous spacing; one primary button bottom-right of the card.

FORM: Brief-pinned (usuário: "deixe ela igual a do assas" — Asaas Checkout register). Concept-seed ran (key a09ae0d8, mode operate); all six rolled challengers (nixie-tube counter, cracktro scroller, starship elbow panel, cassette j-card, tensegrity sculpture, iridescent cloud edge) declined outright — each clashes with Product Principles 1–2 (confiança sem prova social, público advogado sensível a "vendedor genérico"); a decorative retro-console or emissive-void register would read as untrustworthy for a paid legal-content subscription. Pinned direction stands as-is, no raise needed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
